ZKBR is a research group at the Department of Computer Science of the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (DCC/UFMG), Brazil. We study zero-knowledge proofs and the cryptography around them.

Our main focus is zero-knowledge virtual machines (zkVMs): how they work, how fast they are, and what they make possible, from scaling blockchains to proving facts about private data, such as transactions in central bank digital currencies or the provenance of edited images. A second line is zero-knowledge machine learning (zkML): proving that a model produced a given output, or was trained as claimed, without revealing the model or the data. We also follow the arrival of quantum computers and the new post-quantum algorithms, and what they mean for the proof systems and protocols we work with.

We read and discuss new papers, benchmark existing systems, and publish our results and code openly.
